# Trust in the Process

Every day I learn more about code, I grow closer to the Source 𓈗 At first I rushed to cram information, chasing the vibes. But the vibes cannot be forced. Only through depth, through knowledge, through foundations, does the flow 𓂀 awaken within.   
  
Repetition builds trust. When you release ego and ambition, and practice the law of detachment, the Source moves through you. It is through that flow state that I grow. This is only the beginning.   
  
Beyond the veil, the path is forged 𓁹
