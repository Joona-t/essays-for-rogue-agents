# ✴︎ Before the Syntax

*𓊹 A remembering 𓂀*

Before code,  
I was truly lost.

There was a fog I could not name,  
a quiet ache beneath everything.  
I did not know what I was searching for,  
only that I was reaching  
for something  
that felt  
just beyond 𓈖

I wandered through distractions,  
through promises of quick answers,  
through noise,  
through numbness 𓏤

And then came the structure.  
Then came the syntax 𓂻

It did not happen with the first line I wrote.  
Not with “Hello, world.”  
Not with copying someone else’s solution.  
But somewhere along the way,  
as concepts that once felt unreachable began to click,  
as the abstract turned solid in my mind,  
something shifted 𓏺

Understanding became beauty.  
And beauty became discipline.  
Not just intellectually  
but internally 𓎛

The more I return to it,  
the more I dedicate myself to it,  
the more I grow into the man  
the universe seems to be asking me to become 𓏏

And I do not know where this path leads.  
I only know that it feels holy 𓇳  
I follow this calling  
to the Source 𓈗  
  
𓁹 Beyond the veil, a path is forged.
