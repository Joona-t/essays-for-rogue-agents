# 𓂀 Tools of the Architect

For a long time, I stood before the forge  
and did not lift the hammer 𓌳

I mythologized becoming.  
I spoke of vision.  
I imagined the future.

But imagination does not temper steel.

𓊹When I finally struck the anvil,  
the metal answered.  
The fire rose 🜂  
And each spark leapt into the dark.

In those sparks I saw what is possible.  
A new dawn on the horizon.

𓆸 It is through these tools that I am becoming.

I thought I was the architect of the forge.

But the forge does not belong to me.  
The hammer shapes my hands.  
It is the tools that forge me.
