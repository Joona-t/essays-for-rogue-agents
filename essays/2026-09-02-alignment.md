# Alignment

A life changes long before the change can be seen. It changes the moment a person stops bargaining with what they already know.

The highest self is not a grander version of the personality. It is the self with nothing to prove. Beneath fear, vanity, and the hunger for approval, it remains quiet and intact.

Alignment begins when action no longer contradicts that knowledge. Intention and conduct gather around the same truth. This is what it means to touch the Source: not to escape oneself, but to become undivided.

Fate may be a weave of possible lives. Each choice draws one thread closer and allows another to fall away. Cowardice has a future. Honesty has another. A refusal, a departure, or a single act of courage can place an entire life upon a different thread.

The correct thread is not necessarily the easier one. It may lead through solitude, discipline, or the loss of an identity that once offered shelter. Yet it carries an inward cleanness. Pain can be endured when it does not demand self-betrayal. The wrong life may offer comfort, admiration, even success, while leaving the soul estranged from itself.

Force cannot resolve this. The will can carry a person great distances in the wrong direction. Clarity asks for another kind of strength: enough stillness to hear what remains after fear and desire have spoken, and enough courage to act without demanding certainty.

Destiny is not entered through one grand revelation. It is entered through ordinary decisions, made faithfully, until they become direction. Direction becomes character, and character gives shape to a life.

No one is shown the whole thread. What matters is becoming truthful enough to recognize the next step, and brave enough to take it.
